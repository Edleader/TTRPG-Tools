# The System (Core Rules)

This TTRPG system exists for one reason: to determine the mechanics behind the story that is collaboratively being told. The rules are here to resolve uncertainty, keep combat moving, and give characters room to feel distinct. Most of what your character can do lives on cards. This document explains how the world responds when you try something stupid, clever, or violent.

One person is the GM. The rest play Characters. The GM presents the world, portrays its inhabitants, creates situations, and makes rulings. The Characters decide how to explore, what goals to pursue, and how to approach challenges. No one is telling a fixed story — it emerges through play.

---

## Characters at a Glance

Every character is defined by:
- Three stats (determine HP and capability)
- A handful of cards
- A small number of perks gained through levelling

### Stats

| Stat | Covers |
|------|--------|
| Might | Strength, toughness, intimidation, raw physical force |
| Finesse | Agility, precision, speed, coordination, subtlety |
| Mind | Intelligence, awareness, willpower, spells, persuasion |

When you want to do something, explain how you're attempting it. The GM assigns the relevant stat. If the explanation doesn't make sense, the GM may push back. Bullshit creatively.

### Cards

Cards represent weapons, spells, abilities, armour, gear, and anything else. Cards tell you what you can do. Stats tell you how well you do it.

**Active cards** — in your Active Slots on the table; available to use.
**Hand cards** — owned but not currently active. Some can be used directly from hand.

If a card and this document disagree, the card wins.

### Inventory

Passive items — currency, camping gear, non-descript loot — are tracked in your inventory rather than as cards.

## Core Roll Mechanic

Whenever an outcome is uncertain:

1. Note any modifiers from active cards and from the GM
2. Calculate your Target Number (relevant stat ± modifiers)
3. Roll a d20
4. Roll **equal to or under** your Target Number → **success**
5. Roll **above** → **failure**

**Advantage:** roll two d20, take the **lower**
**Disadvantage:** roll two d20, take the **higher**
**Critical success:** rolling a **1** always succeeds
**Critical failures (20):** do not exist

Stats can reach 20, but modifiers always leave a chance to fail.

## System Basics

### Time Passing
Time passes narratively. You can only achieve so much each day. The GM controls the passage of time.

### Resting

**Short Rest** (up to 2 per day, must be separated by combat):
- Restore half your missing HP (round up)
- Restore half your spent Spell Slots (round up)
- Recover any Short Rest abilities

**Long Rest** (once per day — risk of interruption in unsafe locations):
- Restore all HP
- Restore all Spell Slots
- Recover all Short and Long Rest abilities

### Card Management

- **Long Rest:** swap as many cards as you want between hand and active slots
- **Short Rest:** swap half your active cards (round down)
- As specified by any card or perk

## Combat Basics

### Hands

Every character has two hands. Cards list **Hands Required** (1 or 2).
- A 2-hand item requires both hands free
- A 1-hand item occupies one hand while held

### Weapons and Spells

All weapons and spells have a **difficulty penalty**:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|------------|----------|
| Simple | −1 |
| Standard | −2 |
| Complex | −3 (implied) |
| Heavy/Unwieldy | −4 (implied) |

**To attack:** roll under (Stat − Difficulty Modifier)
**On a hit:** roll damage as listed on the card
**Critical hit (roll of 1):** damage = flat + max die + die roll (e.g. 4+d4 becomes 4+4+d4)

**Spell Slots:**
- Base Spell Slots = Mind ÷ 2 (rounded down, max 6)
- Mind 13+: gain 1 additional Spell Slot per character level

A missed attack roll doesn't mean nothing happens — the GM narrates what occurs.

### Damage & Armour

Enemies have no armour class. If you hit, you hit.

Armour **reduces damage after a hit is confirmed**. Armour can never reduce damage to 0 — minimum 1 damage always applies.

*Example: 7 damage vs 2 Armour → 5 damage taken.*

### Abilities

Ability cards are trained techniques. They occupy Active Slots and replace your Primary Action. They typically deal no direct damage — they control space, protect allies, or shift momentum.

### Health (HP)

**Max HP = (Character Level + Might) × 2**

At 0 HP: fall unconscious. Each turn, make a Might roll (disadvantage) to stabilise at 1 HP. Fail three in a row → death.

An ally within 2m may stabilise you with a Mind roll (−5 difficulty; disadvantage if an enemy is within 2m of them). Costs all attacks that turn.

### Movement

Base movement: **14 metres**

On a grid:
- 1 square (1 inch / 2.5 cm) = 2 metres
- Diagonal = 3 metres

Movement can be taken at any point during your turn.

**Ranged weapons:** attack with **disadvantage** when target is within **half** of maximum range.

## Combat Structure

Combat alternates between **Player Phase** and **NPC Phase** until it ends.

If it's unclear who goes first, the GM rolls a d20: **even = players first, odd = NPCs first**.

### Player Phase

Players take turns in any order. Each player must complete their full turn before the next starts.

**On your turn, in any order:**
- One Primary Action
- Use one Hand card (if the card allows it)
- Move up to your movement limit (split between actions as desired)
- Shout something

**Primary Actions (all characters):**
- Attack with one active weapon or spell card
- Unarmed: Might ÷ 4 (round up, minimum 1) damage
- Perform one Combat Manoeuvre
- Use one active Ability card
- Interact with environment (lever, flip table, etc.)
- Double your movement
- Suggest something creative

**Combat Manoeuvres (all characters):**
- **Grapple:** Might roll (GM sets difficulty). Target cannot move until grapple ends. Target may attempt Finesse roll to escape at start of their turn.
- **Shove:** Might roll (GM sets difficulty). Target pushed 2m away and has disadvantage on next attack.

### Attacking

1. Choose attack from a card
2. Apply difficulty modifier and any other modifiers
3. Roll d20 equal to or under target number
4. On success: roll damage, apply armour and effects

### NPC States

States that may affect whether an attack connects. GM will describe why — which is usually a clue for how to counter it.

Examples: Stimulated, Sedated, Shielded, Desperate

### NPC Phase

NPCs generally have available:
- All actions available to them
- One non-combat ability
- Movement up to their limit

### Player States

Cards or perks may allow players to adopt states that make attacks against them harder.

## Character Creation

**Starting points:** 36 stat points distributed across three stats. No stat may exceed 15 at creation.

**Starting slots:**
- 4 Active Card slots
- 4 Hand card slots (max hand size)
- 0 Perks

### Levelling Up

Characters progress from Level 1 to Level 20, gaining stat points, active slots, hand slots, and perks.

*(See character levelling table — to be added)*

Active slots and hand size max out at 9 (unless a card modifies this). Stats max at 20 from points alone (cards may push beyond).

## Perks

Perks are always-on capabilities. If a perk and a card conflict, the card wins.

Perks are gained at specific levels:

### Level 5 — Identity Perks *(choose one)*

| Perk | Requirement | Effect |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Aggressor | Might 14+ | Once per Player Phase: on a Might hit, immediately make one additional attack with the same weapon/spell |
| Keen Eye | Finesse 14+ | Once per Player Phase: on a Finesse hit, immediately make one additional attack with the same weapon/spell |
| Big Brain | Mind 14+ | Once per Player Phase: on a Mind spell hit, immediately cast once more with the same spell |
| Dual Wielder | Any stat 15+, two 1-hand cards active | May dual wield Simple weapons/spells; use both on your action |
| Defender | — | Once per combat: reduce incoming damage by 5 (after armour; may reduce to 0) |
| Tactician | — | Once per combat: expend your Hand card use to freely swap one active and hand card |
| Lucky | — | Once per Long Rest: reroll any d20 you just made; must take the new result |
| Over-prepared | — | Once per Long Rest: declare exceptional preparation; gain advantage on next non-combat roll |
| Inspiring Presence | — | Once per Short Rest: reduce any ally's out-of-combat roll by 2 after they roll |

### Level 10 — Specialisation Perks *(choose one)*

| Perk | Requirement | Effect |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Relentless Aggressor | Might 16+ | Once per Player Phase: ignore the difficulty penalty on a Might attack |
| Exploit Weakness | Finesse 16+ | Critical hit on a roll of 1, 2, or 3 for Finesse attacks |
| Expanding Brain | Mind 16+ | Once per Player Phase: on a failed Mind spell, still impose half the effect (halve, round down) |
| Ambidextrous | Any stat 17+ | Two+ hits on same target this turn: add one extra damage die to second hit. Also unlocks Standard dual wield if Dual Wielder taken |
| Unshakeable | — | Once per combat: if an attack would reduce you to 0 HP, you're reduced to 1 HP instead |
| Veteran | — | Once per combat: use one additional Hand card |
| Fortune Favours the Bold | — | Once per Long Rest: give yourself advantage on any d20 (declare before rolling) |
| Competent Person | — | Critical hit on a roll of 1 or 2 for all attacks |
| Trusted Advisor | — | Once per Short Rest: reduce any ally's roll by 3 after they roll |

### Level 17 — Mastery Perks *(choose one)*

| Perk | Requirement | Effect |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Apex Aggressor | Might 18+ | Once per Player Phase: on a Might hit, charge to another enemy within 6m and immediately attack with the same weapon (no extra movement cost) |
| Surgical Precision | Finesse 18+ | Once per Player Phase: on a Finesse hit, deal ricochet damage to another enemy within 2m of original target (both rolls use base damage; ricochet can't crit) |
| Galaxy Brain | Mind 18+ | Once per Player Phase: declare before rolling a Mind spell. On success: double maximum effect. On miss: turn ends immediately |
| Overwhelming Barrage | Any stat 19+ | Each successive hit this turn adds one extra damage die to the next hit (stacking). Miss = continue with no bonus. Ambidextrous adds +1 die per hit after first. Also unlocks Complex dual wield if Dual Wielder and Ambidextrous taken |
| Unbreakable | — | Once per combat: if an attack would reduce you to 0 HP, you stay at your current HP instead |
| Fate-Bender | — | Once per Long Rest: treat any roll as if you rolled a 1 |
| Failing Upwards | — | Once per Long Rest: if you fail an attack roll by exactly 1, treat it as a critical hit |
| Legendary Leader | — | Once per Long Rest: when an ally fails a roll, you may declare it a success instead |
