# Weapons

Cards are grouped by Generation (roughly every 4 levels). Generation 1 covers levels 1–4.

---

## Generation 1 (Levels 1–4)

**Requirements:** Level 1. Stat listed (10+).
**Stat used** determines the roll. **Difficulty** is the modifier applied to that stat.
**Damage** is rolled on a successful hit. Critical hit (roll of 1): flat + max die + die roll.
