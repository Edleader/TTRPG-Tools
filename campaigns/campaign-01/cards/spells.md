# Spells

Spells are bioelectric manipulation. They require **Mind 10+** and cost **Spell Slots** (see rules). All spells use the **Mind** stat.

Spell scraps — encoded clinical documents from Dr. Maren Voss's pre-anomaly research facility — are how new spells enter play. Charlotte draws them from nightmares. They can also be taken from dead enemies.

---

## Generation 1 (Levels 1–4)

**Requirements:** Level 1, Mind 10+. **Stat:** Mind. **Slots:** as listed.

| Name | Hands | Difficulty | Range | Effect | Slots | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|------------|-------|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| Cauterise | 1H | Simple (−1) | 2m | 2+d6 **healing** | 1 | — | Forces accelerated clotting; seals wounds |
| Clot | 1H | Standard (−2) | 20m | 1+d4 **healing** | 1 | — | Caster takes 4 HP damage on use |
| Vertigo | 1H | Simple (−1) | 20m | — | 1 | — | Target has **Disadvantage** on their next attack |
| Muscle Cramp | 1H | Standard (−2) | 20m | — | 1 | — | Target **cannot move** on their next turn |
| Static Discharge | 1H | Simple (−1) | 20m | 1+d4 | 1 | — | Concentrated bioelectric burst; disorienting |
| Lung Constriction | 2H | Standard (−2) | 10m | 2+d6 | 2 | — | Tightens airways. If uncontrolled: does not stop. Charlotte's death spell. |
| Fever | 1H | Standard (−2) | 20m | 2+d4 | 1 | — | Spikes target's core body temperature |

### GM Note — Lung Constriction
This is the spell Charlotte attempts in Ch6. She cannot control it. It does not end when she releases the scrap. The effect turns inward. This should be described slowly — she looks confused, then frightened, then she cannot speak. It takes approximately 30 real-world minutes to play out. Do not rush it. Do not let it be interrupted by other things happening in the scene.
