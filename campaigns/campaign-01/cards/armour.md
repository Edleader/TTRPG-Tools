# Armour

Armour reduces damage after a hit is confirmed. It **never makes an attack miss**. Minimum damage taken is always 1, regardless of armour value.

Armour covers specific body locations. Multiple pieces can be worn simultaneously if they cover different areas.

---

## Generation 1 (Levels 1–4)

**Requirements:** Level 1.

| Name | DR | Coverage | Value (Marks) | Notes |
|------|----|----------|---------------|-------|
| Padded Jacket | 1 | Upper body | 50 | Thick padded jacket with reinforced shoulders. Not purpose-built but better than nothing |
| Leather Vest | 2 | Upper body | 200 | Heavy leather with additional padding at impact zones. Light enough to move freely |
| Reinforced Cargo Pants | 1 | Legs | 100 | Heavy-duty trousers with reinforced panels at thighs and shins |
| Motorcycle Helmet | 1 | Head | 100 | Full-face. Impact-rated. Considerably better than your skull alone |

**DR** = Damage Reduction (subtracted from incoming damage per hit, minimum 1 damage).
