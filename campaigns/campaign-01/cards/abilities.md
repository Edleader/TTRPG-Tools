# Abilities

Ability cards represent trained techniques and tactical options beyond basic attacks. They occupy Active Slots and are used in place of your **Primary Action**. Unlike weapons and spells, abilities typically deal no direct damage — they control space, protect allies, create openings, or shift momentum.

---

## Generation 1 (Levels 1–4)

**Requirements:** Level 1 (unless noted). **Quantity per deck:** 2 of each.

| Name | Type | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Bull Rush | Primary Action | Move up to 6m (additional to normal movement) in a straight line. On collision with an enemy: Might roll (difficulty −2). Success: enemy pushed 4m back and cannot attack next NPC Phase. Failure: turn ends immediately where you stopped. |
| Hold the Line | Primary Action | For the next three NPC Phases, enemies cannot move past you within 2m. If they attempt to, they stop adjacent to you. |
| Time Slip | Primary Action | Take twice your movement and play two Hand cards this turn. |
| Focussed Disarm | Primary Action | Make a Might roll (difficulty −5) against a target within 2m. Success: knock the weapon from their hands. |
| Taunt | Primary Action | Make a Mind roll (difficulty −3). Success: choose up to 2 enemies within 10m — they focus on you for the next two NPC Phases. |
| Guard | Primary Action | Take a protective stance over an ally within 2m. The next attack that would hit them hits you instead. |
| Shadow Step | Primary Action | Move up to double your full movement. If at any point you are out of view of all enemies, you remain hidden until your next turn. Your next attack is made with **Advantage**. |
