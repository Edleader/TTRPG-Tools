# Items

Items are Hand cards — owned but not in Active Slots. Some can be used directly from hand. Consumables are destroyed on use.

---

## Generation 1 (Levels 1–4)

**Quantity per deck:** 3 of each.

| Name | Slot | Effect | Value (Marks) | Notes |
|------|------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Field Medkit | Hand | Restore **10 HP**. Cannot be used in combat. Consumed on use. | 50 | Compact kit: dressings, antiseptic, basic surgical tools |
| Stimulant | Hand | Restore **d10 HP**. Consumed on use. | 40 | Fast-acting pre-loaded injector. Can be used in combat. |
| Rope | Hand | 30 metres of braided nylon. Climbing, restraining, rigging. | 15 | Reusable |
| Duct Tape | Hand | Field repairs, improvised restraints, structural fixes. | 10 | Reusable roll |
| Lock Picks | Hand | Attempt to bypass locks. GM may call for a Finesse roll (difficulty varies). | 60 | Reusable |
| Tarp | Hand | Large heavy-duty tarpaulin. Shelter, concealment, improvised stretcher. | 12 | Reusable |
| Lighter | Hand | Start fires. Applications range from practical to desperate. | 8 | Reusable until fuel runs out |
| Binoculars | Hand | Identify targets and assess terrain from a safe distance. | 45 | Reusable |
| Magnifying Glass | Hand | Examine fine details, read damaged text, start fires. | 20 | Reusable |

## Currency

**Marks** — small stamped metal fragments minted by The Brokers. The standard post-anomaly currency. Values listed on cards are in Marks.

General trade still happens for food, fuel, components, and anything else people value — but Marks are the hard currency. The Brokers control supply and effectively set exchange rates.
