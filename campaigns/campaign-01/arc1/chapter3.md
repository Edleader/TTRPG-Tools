# Arc 1, Chapter 3 — The Broker / Wider World

## Status: READY TO RUN

## Overview
The morning after Chapter 2. The world gets bigger. The party has a concrete job to do today — Mara needs specific supplies, and she has a lead on where to find them. While they're out, they encounter their first Broker and learn about Marks, witness the early shape of something watching them, and find a fragment that doesn't quite fit. Two new threads open by the end of the session.

---

## Section 1 — The Morning Problem

Mara catches the party before the day starts. She's been up early — she does this, carries the weight of the place before anyone else is awake.

> *"I need medical supplies. Specifically: antiseptic, wound dressings, and if you can find it, anything for infection. We've got one of the older residents running a fever. It's probably nothing, but I'm not equipped for it if it isn't."*

She unfolds a rough hand-drawn map of the surrounding streets — she's been building it from descriptions people have given her since the anomaly. She marks an area a few streets out.

> *"There was a chemist on the high street, and a medical clinic around the corner from it. I don't know what state either is in. Someone told me there's also a man with a van who trades — apparently he's been set up near what used to be the small shopping strip for a few days now. Might be worth talking to him."*

She looks at the party.
> *"I'd go myself, but I can't leave this place. Just bring back what I've asked for."*

**Charlotte moment:** as the party gear up to leave, Charlotte is sitting near the door with her notebook. She looks up at whoever leaves last.

> *"Where are you going today?"*

Whoever answers, she nods and goes back to drawing. She is mapping who comes back.

## Section 2 — Out in the World

### The Chemist

The chemist's shopfront has a smashed window — looting happened early. Most of the front shelves are stripped. But the back dispensary area behind the counter is intact; whoever looted the place didn't know what was worth taking.

**What's here:** antiseptic, wound dressings, disposable gloves, basic pain relief, and — if the party look carefully — a sealed pack of broad-spectrum antibiotics still in a locked glass cabinet (Finesse roll, difficulty −1 to get it open, or just break the glass).

**Complication:** there is someone already in the dispensary when the party enter. A young woman — mid-20s, medical scrubs, hair still tied back from a shift she never finished. She's been sleeping here.

She's not a threat. She's terrified and then relieved. She tells them she was working at the clinic when the surge hit — she's been in the chemist since because it felt safer than going out. If the party are friendly, she'll tell them:
- The clinic around the corner is still structurally intact but she doesn't want to go back.
- There's a man with a van a couple of streets over. *"He came through two days ago. He actually paid me for a box of bandages. Real small metal coins. Strangest thing."*
- She's heard people moving through the area at night. She doesn't know who they are. *"They don't make much noise. They're just there and then not."*

She doesn't want to leave the chemist. If the party invite her to the community centre, she thinks about it, doesn't commit. She might turn up later.

---

### Encounter 1 — The Lookouts

On the way between the chemist and the shopping strip, two people are standing at a street corner doing nothing. When the party pass, one of them makes a note of something in a small pad.

If the party confront them:

> *"We're not doing anything."*

They're not armed visibly. They're not aggressive. They maintain that they're just standing there. One of them has a white symbol on the inside of his jacket collar — visible if someone gets close enough.

If the party push for more:
> *"You're the group from the community centre, aren't you. We've heard about you."*

They walk away. They don't run. They just walk away calmly.

**GM note:** The party are being logged. The Purists have informants and watchers throughout the area. This is not a fight — it's a feeling. They are visible in a way they weren't before. Do not name the Purists yet.

**If the party follow them or try to stop them:**

The two watchers don't engage, but two more emerge from a side street — four total now, enough to make it uncomfortable. They still won't throw the first punch, but the message is clear: there are more of them.

**Watchers (if forced into combat):**
- Might 12, 18 HP each
- **Improvised Weapon (pipe/bottle):** Simple −1, 1+d4 damage
- They disengage and withdraw the moment they take any significant damage. They are not here to fight today.

### The Broker

Parked near the old shopping strip — a converted van, side panel open, makeshift counter with a few organised shelves of scavenged goods. The man running it is relaxed and immediately friendly.

He introduces himself by first name only — *"Dave"* — and has clearly been doing this for a few days. He buys and sells.

> *"What've you got? I'll give you a fair price."*

**He pays in Marks.** If the party haven't heard of Marks:

> *"Small metal stamped bits. The Brokers mint them — that's the network I work through. Controlled supply, everyone knows what they're worth. Better than trading tins of beans every time you want something. You'll want some."*

He's well-informed in the way that comes from talking to everyone who passes through.

If the party engage:
- He can tell them which streets feel safe and which feel contested right now.
- He mentions, neutrally: *"Settlement out east turned out a family a couple of days ago. Kid developed something after the anomaly — one of those charged things. They were asked to leave."* He says it like weather. *"People get scared. Can't blame them, I suppose."* He does not name any group. He doesn't know the name, or doesn't use it.
- If asked about the watchers the party just encountered: *"Yeah, I've seen them. They're tracking something. Wouldn't worry about it."* He clearly would worry about it.

**What he'll buy:** scavenged goods, tools, food surplus, useful finds.

**What he'll sell (at reasonable Marks cost):** rope, duct tape, a lighter, a basic first aid kit, candles, a hand tool or two. He has no weapons — *"that's not really my thing."*

**GM note:** He is a Broker node. He is useful, personable, and has no moral floor. He is not a villain. He is just not constrained by anything other than profit. Both things are true simultaneously.

### Something That Doesn't Fit

Somewhere in the area — on a wall near the medical clinic, or etched into the corner of a shopfront shutter — a small symbol. Not spray paint. Etched or stamped. Deliberate. Easy to miss.

It matches the symbols in Charlotte's notebook.

**GM note:** Do not draw attention to it. If players notice and ask: no one knows what it is. File it. This is the second occurrence of Thread E.

---

### Encounter 2 — The Clinic

The medical clinic around the corner is intact. The front door is unlocked. Inside: examination rooms with locked medication cabinets, a waiting area, a small storeroom.

**What's useful:** additional dressings, a blood pressure cuff, a pulse oximeter, more antiseptic. In the storeroom, an unopened case of saline bags.

**What's in here:** two people who have set up in the waiting area — an older man and a teenage boy. The man is injured — a badly wrapped arm wound, a few days old. They are not hostile. They are frightened and have been here since the day after the anomaly.

The boy says:
> *"There were people here yesterday. They looked around and left. They had these white things on their arms."*

If the party have medical supplies, they can help with the old man's wound (Finesse roll, difficulty −1, or just describe careful treatment). If they do, the man tells them:

> *"There's a woman with a van nearby. She knows more about what's happening than she lets on."* (He means Dave the Broker — he doesn't know the van is run by a man.) *"She said something about a man running a camp further out. Organised. His people don't cause trouble. Didn't catch the name."*

This is the first vague hint of Brad — don't push it.

## Section 3 — Return

**Charlotte moment:** she's been helping fold some donated clothing or stacking something small. When the party come in, she has a new drawing ready. She slides it across the table to whoever sits nearest — more symbols, different arrangement.

> *"You came back."*

Not a question. Just a thing she noticed.
