# Arc 1 — Streets of Chaos

## Overview
The opening arc. Players are fresh out of the anomaly, disoriented, and immediately thrown into survival mode. This arc is about understanding the world, building a base of resources and allies, and slowly realising that something bigger is going on.

The first two sessions are relatively linear — establishing food, shelter, fuel, and basic gear. From Session 3 onward, multiple doors open and players can pursue threads in their own order.

The arc closes with a boss encounter of some kind.

## Themes
- Survival and resource scarcity
- Community vs. self-interest
- Discovery of charged abilities
- The world as it is now
