# Last Session Recap (Ch1 — The Supply Run)

*Read or paraphrase to the table at the start of the next session. Keep it loose.*

## The world, briefly
- The anomaly hit roughly **two years ago** — a global electrical surge that fried most circuitry, killed grids in patches, and dropped people unconscious where they stood.
- Modern electronics are dead. Cars built before the boards-and-chips era still run if you can find fuel. Lights flicker on sometimes, sometimes don't.
- Governments are gone or fractured. Cities are territories. Most people are still figuring out which way is up.
- The party only woke up a few days ago — they are some of the latest known wakers.
- Some people came out of the surge **changed** — small things at first. Static where there shouldn't be. Lights flickering when tempers flare. The world is starting to call them "charged."

## What you did last session

**Waking up at Brad's**
- Came to in Brad's backyard / lounge / wherever you collapsed two years ago. Hangover from the surge, hangover from the night before — hard to tell which was worse.
- The night before the anomaly is mostly a blur. What pieces you do remember include, for reasons no one wants to claim ownership of, **multiple novelty butt plugs that may or may not have been rigged to explode.** No one is in a hurry to revisit that conversation.
- Brad was **gone.** No body, no note, no sign of struggle. Just absent.
- Power flickering. Phones dead. Fridge spoiled. The world outside the window looked like a still photograph.

**To the community centre**
- Walked the street together for the first time. Cars abandoned mid-lane. Boarded shopfronts. The sky a little wrong.
- **Met Billy Cross on the way** — fell in with the group between Brad's place and the community centre. He didn't wake up where you woke up. He had his own reasons for being in the area.
- A helpful stranger pointed you toward the old community centre — *"They're organising people."*
- Met **Mara Singh** (practical, tired, runs the place) and **Darren Pike** (Mara's right hand). About twenty residents inside, mostly elderly or hurt.
- Mara gave you the supply run job — abandoned supermarket, food, possibly hostile occupants. The pay was shelter, food, and a place at the table.
- Communities Centre 'enforcer' called **Resolute**.
- Got to choose **starting weapons** from the centre's storeroom.
- Mara also slid across a small stack of **scrap papers** covered in dense, strange symbols. She didn't know what they were. **Some of you took one look and just... understood it.** Like reading something in a language you didn't know you spoke. The ones you understood became your starting spells. The ones you didn't, you didn't.

**The supply run**
- Took the streets to the supermarket. Met **three scavengers** picking the place over out the front. Fight was clean — you won, moved on, didn't take anything serious back.
- Inside: dim aisles, stripped shelves, useful supplies hidden in the stock rooms. Loaded up backpacks with food, medications, basics.
- A **radio crackled briefly** somewhere in the building. A voice. Then dead again. No one had a great explanation for how a radio with no power could do that.

**The way home**
- Ambushed a few blocks out by **a five-person gang** — four with baseball bats, one leader with an axe and a knack for knocking weapons out of people's hands.
- Fought through them, supplies intact, made it back to the centre.
- Mara accepted the haul without ceremony. The residents got fed. You got a place to sleep.

**Mara's parting words as you wound down for the night:**
> *"Word is spreading about people who came out of the surge… different. Someone nearby is looking for them."*

Then the generator coughed and the lights went out.
