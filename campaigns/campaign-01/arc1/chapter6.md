# Arc 1, Chapter 6 — The Siege / Charlotte

## Status: READY TO RUN

## Overview
The Purists have identified the community centre as a focal point for charged people. They believe Charlotte — the child who has been present since the party arrived — is the source of their powers, or at minimum a conduit for them. They have encircled the building. This chapter runs two things simultaneously: a siege outside, and Charlotte dying inside. The party cannot fully attend to either without neglecting the other. Charlotte's last words prove the Purists wrong. Then the party fights.

**This is the most important chapter of Arc 1. Take your time.**

---

## Before the Session

Read the Lung Constriction entry in spells.md. Understand what the spell does biologically. You will need to describe it slowly and accurately.

Charlotte has had four chapters of small moments. If those were played well, the players are attached without knowing it. If they were rushed, give this chapter a little extra space at the start — a final quiet moment before it tips.

---

## Section 1 — Morning: The Siege Tightens

The people from last night are still there. There are more of them now. They have spread to cover the side street. No one has slept particularly well.

Mara is at the window when the party are up.

> *"They haven't rushed the door. They've been there all night — I checked at two, I checked at four. They're not going anywhere."*

She turns from the window.

> *"They're waiting for something."*

They are waiting for the people inside to come out.

**What the Purists look like:** organised but not military. Civilian clothes with identifying marks — white armbands, or a symbol painted on a jacket. Most carry improvised weapons. A few have better gear. One has a hunting rifle, visible but not raised. They are not a mob. They have a leader and they are following instruction.

**The leader** stands at the front of the group. He is in his 50s, stocky, carries himself like someone who has spent years being certain he was right and has now been proven correct by the end of the world.

**Charlotte** is in the building. She has been awake for a while. She is sitting in her corner, notebook closed on her lap.

---

## Section 2 — The Speech

The Purist leader speaks to the building. Loudly. Calmly. This is prepared. The street goes quiet when he starts.

**Deliver this speech. Do not rush it. Let it land.**

> *"We know what's in there. We know what you've done. We're not angry people. We're people who understand what happened and what it means.*
>
> *That surge didn't give people powers. Something caused it. Something that was already in the world. A contamination. And we've traced it. We know where it comes from.*
>
> *There's a child in there. She arrived after the anomaly. She's been with you since your people woke up. Since before, maybe. And every charged person in that building has been in contact with her.*
>
> *We're not asking you to hurt her. We're asking you to hand her over. She'll be safe. We just need to remove the source.*
>
> *You have one hour."*

The speech is wrong about everything. It is also delivered with complete sincerity by a man who absolutely believes every word.

**Charlotte hears this.** She is sitting very still when the party find her. Her notebook is in her lap. She has been listening.

She looks up when someone comes close, and then looks back at the window.

> *"They think it's me."*

Not a question.

---

## Section 3 — The Hour

The party have an hour. Let them use it — planning, talking to Mara, checking what Darren has managed to barricade. Give the hour some texture: the weight of being watched, the quiet inside, the occasional movement visible through the windows.

Mara moves the residents to the back rooms. Darren does what he can with furniture and boards.

**Charlotte finds whoever she's closest to.**

She has the Lung Constriction scrap. Her copy — the one she would not give up. She's been carrying it since Chapter 2. She has been thinking about this for the entire time.

> *"I want to help. I know what I did wrong last time. I've been thinking about it."*

She does not wait for a response. She tries.

---

## Section 4 — How Charlotte Dies

**Run this slowly. This is approximately 30 real-world minutes of play.**

The effect begins. She looks focused. And then something shifts.

She drops the scrap. Looks at her hands.

The effect does not stop.

> *Her breathing changes. It's audible. She tries to say something and it doesn't come out right. She sits down — not dramatically, just her legs went. She looks up at whoever is nearest. She is frightened. She is trying very hard not to show that she is frightened. She is nine years old. She has been trying not to need help since her parents died.*

**The party can try everything.** They should try everything. That is the point.

- **Grabbing the scrap** — it's already on the floor. That's not how it works.
- **Grabbing her** — she doesn't resist, but it doesn't help.
- **Mind rolls to counter** — the GM may allow partial slowing (difficulty −5). It cannot fully stop it. The spell has inverted inward and there is no external loop to break.
- **Healing spells (Cauterise, Clot)** — they don't address the mechanism. Her lungs are constricting. The spell is biological.
- **Just talking to her** — she can hear them. She cannot answer easily. But she can hear them.

**What she says, in fragments, between attempts to breathe:**

> *"I didn't...* [breath] *...cause it."*

> *"I was just...* [breath] *...watching you sleep."*

> *"I didn't mean to...* [breath] *...make you wake up late."*

She doesn't fully understand what she is saying. She has pieced together something in the time she has been here — fragments she overheard, things that didn't make sense until now. She is trying to tell them something true in the time she has.

**She goes quiet.** Not suddenly. Just quieter, and then still. She is sitting against the wall. Her notebook is beside her.

**The drawing on the wall:** all of them. It has been there for several sessions. It is there now. It will be there when they come back from wherever Arc 2 takes them. It will never be mentioned directly again.

## Section 5 — The Fight

The Purists don't know what just happened inside. They know their hour is up.

They move on the community centre.

**The party is fighting people who just killed Charlotte by being wrong.** The Purists don't know they killed her. They believe they were right. That is worse.

---

### The EMP Device

The Purists have built a crude electromagnetic pulse device — salvaged capacitors, scratch-built from components. It discharges once at the start of the fight, before anyone has acted.

**EMP — Effect:**
All charged party members make a Mind roll (difficulty −3). On a failure, their spell slots are temporarily suppressed — they cannot cast spells for the first two NPC phases of combat. The effect wears off after two NPC phases. The device is destroyed after use. It cannot be used again.

**Describe it:** a box with a coil, discharged with a battery. It makes a low-frequency thump. The effect should feel physical — a pressure behind the eyes, a static feeling in the chest — before the spell slots go quiet.

**After the fight:** the device can be examined. Players can learn from it. The Purists were not stupid. They were wrong.

---

### Purist Force Composition

**Wave 1 — Outer group (engage first):**

| Name | Might | HP | Weapon | Damage |
|------|-------|----|--------|--------|
| Purist Member ×6 | 12 | 20 | Baseball Bat / Pipe (Simple −1) | 2+d4 |

**Wave 2 — Inner group (arrive round 3, led by the leader):**

| Name | Might | HP | Weapon | Damage | Armour |
|------|-------|----|--------|--------|--------|
| Purist Veteran ×3 | 14 | 25 | Hatchet / Combat Baton (Standard −2) | 2+d4 or 1+d6 | Leather Vest: DR 2 |
| Purist Leader | 15 | 35 | Lumber Axe, two-handed (Standard −2) | 2+d6 | Leather Vest: DR 2 |

**Leader's Ability — Focussed Disarm:**
- **Action:** Primary Action
- **Effect:** Might roll (difficulty −5) against a target within 2m. On success: the target's weapon is knocked from their hands. Target must use their next Primary Action to retrieve it or fight unarmed.
- **When he uses it:** He targets the most visibly powerful party member — whoever used a spell, or whoever has done the most damage so far.

**Leader's speech mid-fight** (if he gets close enough):

> *"You don't have to keep fighting. Just leave. The child is gone. Whatever she was doing, it stops now. Just walk away."*

He genuinely believes this. He genuinely believes Charlotte was the source. He will never know he was wrong.

---

### The Fight Itself

The battlefield is the community centre — the front area, the corridor, possibly the entrance and the small space outside. Mara and the residents are sheltering in the back rooms.

The Purists fight to drive the charged people out, not to kill. But they will hurt people seriously if resisted. The distinction stops mattering quickly.

---

## Section 6 — After

The Purists are driven off, or enough of them are hurt that they withdraw. The leader leaves — not running, walking, with the particular dignity of someone who has decided this is not over.

The community centre is intact.

**What the players are left with:**

Charlotte is still against the wall. The notebook is beside her. The drawing is on the wall above her.

Mara comes out from the back. She sees. She doesn't say anything for a long moment.

> *"She didn't do anything wrong."*

That's all she says. She goes to find something to cover her with.

**The drawing stays on the wall.** No one takes it down. No one says to leave it up. It just stays.

## Into Arc 2

The party have a car. They have a reason to move that isn't just curiosity.

Brad is out there. He has been out there since the beginning. He left the morning after the anomaly. He has time and resources on them. He has a camp and followers.

The world is bigger than this building. Charlotte wanted to help them be part of it.
