# Arc 1, Chapter 4 — Something Worth Protecting

## Overview
The morning after Chapter 3. The community centre faces a direct threat and has to be defended. Charlotte witnesses a party member use a spell. She tries to use the Lung Constriction scrap she has been keeping — it backfires. This is the chapter where the players stop thinking of the community centre as somewhere they are staying and start thinking of it as theirs. Brad's name surfaces for the first time.

---

## Section 1 — The Morning Problem

Mara finds the party early. She has a folded piece of paper in her hand. Her jaw is tight.

> *"Another one came under the door overnight."*

She unfolds it and puts it on the table. Plain paper, hand-written in careful block letters:

**REPORT WHAT YOU SEE. WE ARE WATCHING.**

> *"That's the second one. First one came two days ago — I didn't say anything because I didn't want to worry people. But this morning Darren saw a group at the end of the street before dawn. Five or six of them. Just standing there. They left before anyone else was up."*

She doesn't say she's frightened. She is frightened.

> *"I need someone to go and have a look. Scout the surrounding streets — I want to know if there are more of them, and whether they're still around. And if you can find out what they want, do that too."*

**Charlotte moment:** She's in her corner. She heard all of it. She has stopped drawing. She is sitting very still, looking at the note on the table.

---

## Section 2 — Scouting the Streets

The party head out to check what's happening in the surrounding streets. The community centre is in a residential block — houses, a few small commercial buildings, a park.

**What they find:**

Three people are positioned at different points around the block. Not together — spread out, covering angles. Two are on foot. One is sitting on a front step pretending to read. They share the same identifying mark: a white symbol drawn on the inside of a jacket collar, or a white armband tucked up under a sleeve. Visible if you're close enough. Invisible if you're not.

They are watching the community centre.

**If the party approach one of them:**

The man on the step looks up, perfectly calm.

> *"Lovely morning."*

If pressed:

> *"We're just spending time in the neighbourhood. Public street."*

If the party make clear they know what's happening:

> *"That building has a problem. The people inside are a problem. Not you specifically — we don't know you. But the things coming out of that place are going to cause damage to everyone around here. We're keeping an eye on it. That's all."*

He is not aggressive. He is absolutely certain he is correct. He will not be reasoned with on the core belief, but he can be persuaded that today is not the day for escalation.

**If the party refuse to back down and it becomes a fight:**

The three watchers produce weapons — they came prepared for this possibility.

**Purist Watchers (3):**
- Might 12, 18 HP each
- **Steel Pipe:** Simple −1, 2+d4 damage
- They call for backup after the first round — two more arrive from the end of the street at the start of round 2 (same stats). They fight to push the party back toward the community centre, not to kill. They disengage if they lose two of their number.

---

## Section 3 — The Threat Arrives

Whether the party confronted the watchers or simply reported back to Mara, the situation escalates by mid-afternoon. The group from the end of the street is now at the front of the community centre — five people, organised, carrying improvised weapons. The leader is at the front.

He is a man in his 40s — fit, controlled. He carries a length of reinforced pipe held loosely at his side. He knocks on the door. When it opens:

> *"We've been patient. The people with abilities in this building are going to be asked to leave. Not the others — just the ones who came out of the surge different. If they go quietly, we go quietly. If not, we'll make sure they go."*

He means it. He is not a thug. He is a frightened man with a fully formed ideology and the resolve to act on it.

**Options for the party:**

- **Talk him down:** The leader can be argued with, warned off, or threatened. He holds his ground on the core belief but can be convinced today is not the right day. This requires a Might or Mind roll (difficulty −3) and something specific said — a vague argument won't move him. A direct, credible threat backed by demonstrated ability might. If the party successfully send him off, he leaves without the group — but he will be back.

- **Fight:** If negotiation fails, or the party want to take the initiative, it becomes a fight.

**Leader's Ability — Focussed Disarm:**
- **Action:** Primary Action
- **Effect:** Might roll (difficulty −5) against a target within 2m. On success: the target's weapon is knocked from their hands. Target must use their next Primary Action to retrieve it or fight unarmed.
- **When he uses it:** The leader targets whoever has used a spell or the most visibly powerful-looking party member first.

**During the fight — a player uses a spell.** Charlotte sees it from wherever she is sheltering. Her eyes go wide. She sees what the scrap she has been carrying can do.

---

## Section 4 — Charlotte's First Attempt

The group has been driven off. The community centre held. In the relief, in the noise of checking on residents, Charlotte finds whoever was using spells.

She has the Lung Constriction scrap — her copy, the one she would not give up. She's been studying it since she drew it.

> *"I think I know how to do it. I watched you. I want to help."*

She does not wait for an answer. She tries.

**What happens:**

The effect begins. She immediately looks wrong — the concentration on her face shifts into something panicked. She drops the scrap. The effect does not stop. Her hands go to her throat. She makes a sound she is clearly trying not to make.

**The party can stop this.** Unlike later, this is stoppable — she has not gone far enough in.

- **Break her concentration physically:** grab her, shout her name, physically disrupt her. Describe it as breaking the bioelectric loop — not violent, just sudden enough to snap her focus.
- **Counter with a spell:** Mind roll (difficulty −2) from a spell user to ground or counter the effect.
- **Just talk to her:** fast, specific, her name, something real. Her focus breaks. The effect stops.

The effect stops. She sits down hard. Breathing rough but recovering.

> *"I'm sorry."*

She means it. She wanted to help. She is nine years old and she tried to do something useful and it failed and she is deeply embarrassed and frightened and still attempting to look like she is fine.

**Do not let this moment be rushed.** The fight is over. Everyone is okay. There is time to sit with her. What the players say to her now matters. She will remember it.

**If asked if she's okay:**
> *"I'm fine."*

She is not fine. She will be fine. She is nine.

---

## Section 5 — The Quiet After

The community centre is intact. The threat has been driven off for today.

**Brad's name surfaces:** Mara, clearing up after the chaos, mentions something she heard from one of the residents who came through recently — a man passing between areas.

> *"Apparently there's someone running a well-supplied camp somewhere further out. Keeps to himself, doesn't bother anyone. One of the men who came through here said the name was Brad. Is that someone you know?"*

She moves on without waiting for an answer. She has too much to do. But the question hangs.

**Charlotte moment (end of chapter):** she goes back to her corner. She picks up her notebook. She doesn't draw symbols this time. She draws the community centre — the room, the table, the people in it. She doesn't say anything about it. She just draws.

---

## End of Chapter

**What the players should feel:**
- This place is theirs to protect. They chose it.
- Charlotte tried to do something she couldn't do yet. She needed them. They were there.
- Brad is alive.
