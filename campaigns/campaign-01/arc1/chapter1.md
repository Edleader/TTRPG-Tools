# Arc 1, Chapter 1 — The Supply Run

## Status: COMPLETE (Session played)

## Overview
Wake up. Explore the neighbourhood. Find the community centre. Accept the supply mission. Choose starting gear. Travel to the supermarket. Deal with scavengers. Loot the building. Fight a gang on the way back. Return home.

---

## Section 1 — Waking Up

### Scene 1 – The Blackout Hangover
Characters wake up at Brad's house. The night was already a blur before the surge. Brad is nowhere to be found — not even a trace.

Each player describes where they wake up and what they notice first. Ask how they knew Brad, and how they came to be at his place.

**Sensory cues:**
- Phones dead
- Power flickers occasionally
- Electronics smell burnt
- Emergency sirens in the distance
- Sky slightly hazy

**Possible early oddities (don't explain — just hint):**
- Light bulb flickers when a player gets angry
- Static shocks stronger than normal
- Phone briefly turns on then dies

### Scene 2 – Immediate Problem
The house feels disconnected. Fridge warm. Taps weak. Internet dead.

**Food check:** Most fresh food has spoiled. Scraps remain — crackers, warm beer, expired yoghurt, one can of beans. Enough for today. Not sustainable.

If players stall: *"The house tells you everything you need to know. The only way to figure out what's really happening is to see what the rest of the street looks like."*

### Scene 3 – Outside
> *"The street looks like time stopped halfway through the day. Cars sit crooked across lanes, doors hanging open. A few older vehicles rumble somewhere in the distance. Several shop fronts have shattered windows or rough boards nailed across them."*

**Street encounters (pick as needed):**
- **A Man With Too Much to Carry** — shopping trolley piled high, avoids eye contact. Defensive if approached, may draw a kitchen knife.
- **The Burned Car** — sedan, hood blackened, still smells of burnt plastic. *Context: some electronics failed violently.*
- **Rooftop Watcher** — figure ducks behind AC unit. Watching for threats or opportunities. If players linger, watcher may signal friends.
- **The Barricaded Corner Store** — shelves stripped. Looting has happened.
- **The Running Engine** — battered old pickup outside a mechanic's. One of the few vehicles still running.

**Helpful civilian:** *"If you're looking for help, go to the old community centre. They're organising people. Best be careful on your way."*

### Scene 4 – The Community Centre
Generator-powered lights. Partially barricaded entrance. Two figures at the door.

**Mara Singh** — practical, tired, direct. Doesn't trust easily. *"If you're here, you either help or you move on."*
**Darren Pike** — Mara's right-hand. *"What she says goes."*

Inside: ~20 people, mostly elderly or injured. Whiteboard lists supplies (Food, Water, Medicine — most circled or underlined).

**What Mara tells them:** Surge knocked out most electronics. Power grids partially work. Cities fragmenting. Supplies running out. *"Food's the first thing to disappear in a collapse."*

**The job:** Abandoned supermarket taken over by a family of preppers (siblings). Dangerous — scavenger gangs, violence over food. Reward: food, shelter.

**Weapon selection:** Before they leave, Mara unlocks a storage room. Players choose starting weapons.

**Spell introduction:** Mara mentions people waking up able to do things — move objects, spark heat, strange instincts. She slides across rough notes on cards. Players pick starting spells if they want.

**End of scene hook:** *"I hope this group manages to make it back."*

## Section 2 — The Supply Heist

### Scene 1 – Travelling
Move through damaged streets. Roll or choose encounters:
- Burned-out traffic jam
- Abandoned police checkpoint
- Looted convenience store
- Rooftop watcher

**Small loot opportunities:** flashlight, backpack, canned food.

### Scene 2 – First Hostile Encounter (outside supermarket)
3 scavengers already looting. Unorganised — just desperate.

**Possible outcomes:** negotiation, intimidation, fight, stealth around them.

**Dialogue:** *"Back off. This place is ours."*

**Stats:** Unarmed. 2 punches per attack (d4 each). Must roll under 13 to hit. 20 HP each.

### Scene 3 – Inside the Building
All empty trolleys still stacked. Dark aisles. Alarms dead. Shelves thin but stock rooms have supplies.

**Challenges:**
- Locked entrance (force / find key / pry open)
- Noise may attract others

**Twist:** Something weird happens. Radio briefly crackles with a voice. NPC scavenger: *"Did you see that?"* Reinforces charged people concept.

### Scene 4 – The Gang (travel home)
5-person ambush:
- 4 × Baseball bats (2+d4). Roll under 13 to hit. 20 HP each.
- 1 × Axe (2+d6). Roll under 14 to hit. 25 HP. Has **Focussed Disarm** ability.

Players decide: fight, negotiate, or escape with supplies.

**Loot to carry back (players choose what fits in backpacks):**
- Canned beans, rice, pasta, protein bars
- Basic medications, batteries, rope, tarps, duct tape

### Scene 5 – Return
People relieved. Supplies distributed. Trust gained.

**End of chapter hook (GM reads):**
> *"Word is spreading about people who came out of the surge… different."*
> *"Someone nearby is looking for them."*
> *(The generator sputters out. The lights go off.)*
