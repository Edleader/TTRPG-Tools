# Bradeep Nkosi ("Brad")

## At a Glance
- Mixed descent: Indian mother, African father
- Mutual friend (or once removed) of everyone in the party
- Always the butt of jokes / punching bag of the group
- Survived the anomaly but did **not** gain any powers
- Has disappeared since the night of the surge

## Arc Role
**Big Bad of Arc 2.**

Brad survived the anomaly but came out the other side with nothing — no powers, while others around him (possibly including the party) manifested abilities. Having always been the group's punching bag, this broke something in him. He has gone off the deep end, seeking control and power through other means. His arc is about manipulation and a slow reveal — early encounters should leave players unsure whether he is a victim or a villain.

## Personality (pre-anomaly)
- Bit of a douche
- Socially awkward, tried too hard
- Generally harmless but grating

## Notes
- His parents are the villains of Arcs 3 and 4 respectively (order TBD)
- Players may encounter rumours of him before they see him
