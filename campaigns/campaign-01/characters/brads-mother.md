# Brad's Mother

*(Surname Nkosi by marriage. Personal name TBD — fold in once it surfaces in play.)*

## At a Glance
- Indian descent. Married into the Nkosi family.
- **Fanatically protective of Brad.** Loves him absolutely. Expresses it by trying to destroy anything that hurt him.
- Organised, deliberate, well-resourced. Not a cartoon villain.
- A mother who is **catastrophically wrong about how to help her son.**

## Role
**Big Bad of Arc 3.** She arrives because Brad ran back to her broken and humiliated after the Arc 2 confrontation. She wants to fix him — which in her case means eliminating the people who hurt him. She doesn't bluster. She acts.

Arc 3 establishes her as a threat (around Ch15) and confronts her at the arc closer (Ch18). After her defeat, she and Brad flee to the father.

## Personality
- **Smothering, absolute, unyielding** in her care for Brad. Her "best" for him crowds out all else.
- Composed in the way of someone who has already made up her mind. Not loud. Not theatrical.
- Cold to anyone outside the family circle. Warm and devastatingly attentive within it.
- Does not monologue. Acts.

## Allies and Resources
- May find common cause with **The Purists** — their ideology about charged people aligning with her grief about what "those people" did to Brad. Not a faction member; a willing ally of convenience.
- Brings real resources: people, supply, intelligence, location of party operations.

## Why she is dangerous
- She is not improvising. She has thought this through.
- She is not motivated by ideology, ambition, or greed. She is motivated by **love** — the most stubborn fuel of any villain in the campaign.
- She does not see Brad's flaws. She sees only what was done to him.

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- Whether she knew about the anomaly or her son's role / connection to it before it happened — open. Possible breadcrumb in Arc 3.
- Whether she stays alive after Arc 3 closes is open. Defeat does not require death.

## Voice and tone
- Quiet, deliberate, controlled.
- The horror is in her certainty. She is not wrong because she's evil. She is wrong because she **will not stop.**
- Speaks of Brad in the present tense even after he has done terrible things. *"My son needs help. You are the help he needs to be free of."*

## Notes
- Develop her name and specific personal details once Arc 2 has played out and we know more about Brad's pre-anomaly life.
- Understanding **how Brad became what he became** — through her — is part of what makes Arc 3 uncomfortable.
