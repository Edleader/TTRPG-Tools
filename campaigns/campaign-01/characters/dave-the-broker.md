# Dave (The Broker)

## At a Glance
- Operates a converted van as a mobile trading post.
- First-name only. *"Dave."* Doesn't volunteer a surname.
- Relaxed, immediately friendly. **No moral floor.**
- Pays in **Marks** — small stamped metal fragments minted by The Brokers.
- Knows everything that's happening in the area without being asked.
- *"What've you got? I'll give you a fair price."*

## Role
**Party's first Broker contact.** Introduced Ch3 (Arc 1). His function in the campaign is to:
- Establish **The Brokers** as a faction without naming them as villains.
- Introduce **Marks** as the new economy.
- Be a recurring, reliable, **morally neutral** trade contact whose information the party comes to trust.
- Plant faction breadcrumbs (Purists, Renewed) as casual conversation.

He is not a villain. He is just **not constrained by anything other than profit.** Both things are true simultaneously.

## Known Details
- Trades in scavenged goods, tools, food surplus, useful finds.
- Sells common gear — rope, duct tape, lighters, basic first aid kits, hand tools. **No weapons** — *"that's not really my thing."*
- Pays in Marks. Will take Marks. Explains the system to anyone who hasn't heard of it.
- Well-informed about contested streets, growing settlements, groups to avoid. He doesn't offer this as intelligence — it just comes out in conversation.
- Has clearly been doing this for years. The van is set up for it.

## How he handles the party
- Friendly without being effusive.
- Will buy what they have. Will not ask where it came from.
- If asked about the watchers / Purists: *"Yeah, I've seen them. They're tracking something. Wouldn't worry about it."* He clearly would worry about it.
- If asked about the expelled-family rumour: tells it like weather. *"People get scared. Can't blame them, I suppose."* Does not name a faction.
- Will sell out the party if the price is right. **They should not feel this immediately.**

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- He is one node in a citywide Broker network. There are others. Routine intelligence flows back to the Brokers as a whole.
- The Brokers are **economically dominant** in the city. They mint the currency. They effectively function as a central bank. Their reach is bigger than any individual node lets on.
- Dave reports back. He reports honestly. The Brokers know who the party is by the time they leave Ch3.
- He may show up again in **Arc 2 (Ch7 onward)** with paid work — retrieval, delivery, negotiation into contested territory.

## Voice and tone
- Warm. Conversational. Practiced.
- The kind of person who remembers your name and uses it.
- The kind of person who is paying attention to everything you say.

## Notes
- The van itself is a useful recurring set piece. Side panel open, makeshift counter, organised shelves. Same setup every time he appears.
- If the party push him hard about the Brokers' structure, he will deflect amiably: *"Mate, I just trade. I don't run the place."*
