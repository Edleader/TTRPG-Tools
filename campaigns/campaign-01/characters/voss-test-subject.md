# Voss's Test Subject

*(No name yet. May not have one. The party may end up giving them one.)*

## At a Glance
- **Dr. Maren Voss's primary test subject.**
- Survived her research with **extreme, unstable, catastrophically powerful abilities.**
- Not evil in the way Brad is evil. May not have much **agency** left at all.
- The first time the party witnesses the **full scope** of what Voss's research produced.
- A preview of what Voss wanted to create at scale.

## Role
**Arc 6 Boss.** The fight that comes before the final confrontation with Voss. Mechanically: a fight unlike anything before — not because of raw numbers, but because of what they represent.

The party reaches the research facility (or a deeper section of it) in Arc 6 and finds the test subject still active, still in or near the facility, still **becoming**.

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- Whether they are conscious in any meaningful sense is open. May be a person to save. May be a force to stop. May be both.
- **They are what Voss's research produces at its furthest extent.** Defeating them does not end the threat — it confirms it.
- May be **someone the players meet earlier** in a different state, recognised only retrospectively. Plant carefully if you go this route.
- Connection to **Charlotte** — Charlotte was passively primed by the surge while in proximity. The test subject was actively shaped by Voss directly. Two ends of the same spectrum. Charlotte died of a fragment of the work; the test subject became its embodiment.

## Personality
- **Open.** Whether they retain personhood is a creative choice for when Arc 6 lands.
- If they retain a person inside: tragic, partial, fragmentary speech. Possibly still human in moments. Possibly not.
- If they don't: a force, a phenomenon, a presence rather than a character.

## Tone for the encounter
- Not a triumphant boss fight. **A horror-tinged fight that lands as confirmation of what the party has been chasing for 30+ chapters.**
- The party should leave this fight understanding they are not facing a person in Voss — they are facing **a thesis**, of which this subject is the first proof.

## Notes
- Develop this character once Arc 5 is settled. Their specifics may be shaped by which faction outcomes survive Arc 5 and what the party knows by then.
- **Aftermath of this fight:** Voss is aware the party is coming. The final arc begins.
