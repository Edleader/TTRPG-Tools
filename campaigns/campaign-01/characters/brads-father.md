# Brad's Father

*(Surname Nkosi. Personal name TBD — fold in once it surfaces in play.)*

## At a Glance
- African descent, **thick accent.**
- **Emotionally withholding.** Where the mother loved Brad too much, the father loved him not enough. *"Why are you so useless."*
- Quieter, stranger villain than his wife.
- Works through proxies. Dismantles rather than attacks.
- Treats the party as an inconvenience to be managed, not an enemy to be feared.

## Role
**Big Bad of Arc 4.** Brad and his mother flee to him after Arc 3. He receives them with contempt — Brad with disappointment, his wife as a problem returned to him. He does not rant. He does not threaten. He gets to work.

Where the mother attacked head-on with passion, the father **dismantles the party's support structures** through proxies — community centre, allies, supply lines. He underestimates the party throughout the arc, which is how he loses.

## Personality
- Cold, contemptuous, dismissive.
- Treats his son as a failure project. Has done so since Brad was a child.
- Does not raise his voice. He does not need to.
- Operates at scale and at distance. Rarely puts himself in physical danger.
- Believes himself above the people around him — including his own family.

## Methods
- **Works through proxies.** The party rarely meets him directly until late in the arc.
- Disrupts logistics rather than fights battles. Cuts supply lines. Pays informants. Buys influence.
- Uses Brad as a tool when needed and discards him when not. **Brad reappears in Arc 4 reluctantly working alongside his father, still broken, still has not healed.**
- Faction tensions during Arc 4 may be ones he is deliberately stoking.

## Why he is dangerous
- He has resources the mother did not.
- He is patient. He plays a longer game.
- He sees the party as a nuisance. **Underestimating them is the lever the party uses against him.**

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- His attitude toward Brad's mother and toward Brad himself is colder than the mother could see, and the party may witness this in a way that adds to the tragedy of Brad's whole shape.
- His own connection to the anomaly or pre-anomaly events is open — possible breadcrumb that he had business interests around Voss's facility, or knew of it.
- Whether Brad survives Arc 4 is up to play. The arc closes with the family thread settled, but Brad may simply **be somewhere out there**, broken across three arcs, not dead.

## Voice and tone
- Soft-spoken. Heavily accented. Long pauses.
- Does not insult — observes. *"You are still here. I had assumed you would have moved on by now."*
- The contempt is in what he doesn't say, not what he does.
