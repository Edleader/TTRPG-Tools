# Dr. Maren Voss

## At a Glance
- The campaign's **true final villain.**
- Caused the anomaly through dangerous bioelectric research. The event was not natural — it was an experiment that malfunctioned at a catastrophic scale.
- Diminished by what happened, but alive, recovering, and still working toward her original goal.
- Clean, clinical, vaguely Germanic. Sounds like someone who publishes papers and accepts awards.
- Not a monster in self-presentation. A scientist with a thesis she still believes in.

## Role
**Campaign Big Bad.** The final confrontation of Arc 7. Her presence runs the entire length of the campaign as **Thread E** — breadcrumbs from Arc 1 onward, surfacing properly in Arc 5, fully named at the research facility (Ch22), confronted in Arc 7.

Her research was into **modification of human physiology through bioelectric stimulation.** The anomaly was an experiment that escaped containment. Whatever she built to cause it was partially destroyed in the event itself. She has spent the years since the surge recovering capability and continuing the work.

## Known Details (in-character — surfaces over time)
- Pre-anomaly: ran a privately-funded research facility somewhere in or near the city. Location partially destroyed by the surge, partially intact.
- The **spell scraps** scattered across the city are her work — clinical documentation of bioelectric stimulation procedures, encoded symbolically so they could be distributed without being understood by the wrong people.
- One player character (Billy Cross) was actively tracking her in the weeks before the anomaly due to the dangerous nature of her research. He has fragments of intelligence, a name, a face — but no full address, and he understood early that going in alone was suicide.
- Some surviving documents from the facility may bear her name or signature.

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- The anomaly was deliberate. Not a natural phenomenon. Not a meteor. Not a virus. **It was caused by her.**
- The "charged" people — those who came out of the surge with abilities — are unintended consequences of her work, not its goal. The bioelectric surge interacted with latent capacity in some people and activated it.
- Her **original goal** is something more specific and more disturbing than the anomaly itself produced. To be defined when Arc 7 lands. The answer should make a terrible kind of sense.
- Her **primary test subject** survived the research with extreme, unstable abilities and is the **Arc 6 boss.** That fight is a preview of what Voss wanted to create at scale.
- Voss's connection to **Charlotte** is not direct contact — but the symbols Charlotte draws originate from her facility. Charlotte's neurological priming during the surge made her a passive receiver of fragments of that work.

## Personality
- Clinical, unflappable, articulate.
- Genuinely believes in her thesis. Not a cackling villain — a **brilliant scientist who doesn't see the cost.**
- Treats catastrophe as a research outcome to be analysed, not a tragedy to be answered for.
- Does not see "charged" people as her achievement. They are noise on the curve. Her real work is something more deliberate.

## Voice and tone
- Educated, measured, almost soothing.
- Refers to the anomaly as "the event" or "the incident" — never "my work."
- Will discuss what happened with the affect of someone reading from an abstract.
- The horror is in the calmness, not the rhetoric.
