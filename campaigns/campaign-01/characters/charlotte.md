# Charlotte

## At a Glance
- ~9 years old. Small. Acts unbothered. Is not unbothered.
- Lost both parents in the anomaly — watched them die through the window of their car.
- Has a battered notebook. Draws constantly.
- Carries a quiet authority that doesn't fit a child her age.
- *"I drew it from a dream. I don't know what it is."*

## Role
**Arc 1 emotional payload.** Charlotte is the load-bearing emotional thread of Arc 1. She arrives Ch2, is woven into every chapter Ch3–5 with at least one small moment, and **dies in Ch6** — quietly, over ~30 minutes of play, when her second attempt at Lung Constriction does not stop. Her death is the gut punch the entire arc has been building toward without telegraphing.

She does not have powers. She has been **neurologically primed** by exposure to the anomaly while sheltering in her parents' car (a partial Faraday cage). The dampening effect of the cage radiated outward and is the reason the party slept for two years instead of weeks — she has unknowingly been the cause of their late waking. **The party do not know this.** It is revealed in the final arcs.

## Known Details (in-character)
- Says she was "near the house with the dead garden on the corner" — that is Brad's house. She has been following the party at a distance since they left it.
- Drew a spell scrap from a dream: **Lung Constriction.** Functional. Identical to the printed scraps the community centre had. She has a second copy in her notebook.
- Sees the symbols when she closes her eyes. Can redraw them from memory.
- Will not give up the second copy. *"It's mine. I can always draw it again anyway."*
- Asks who comes back at the end of each day. She is mapping who returns.

## Hidden Truths (GM only)
- She survived the surge inside her parents' car. The car partially shielded her. The same effect kept the party dormant for ~2 years instead of weeks.
- She is not "charged." She is **primed** — a different category of affected person, by accident rather than physiology. She doesn't know this. Nobody does, yet.
- The symbols she draws are clinical bioelectric stimulation procedures, originally produced by **Dr. Maren Voss's** pre-anomaly research facility. She is, neurologically, a passive receiver of fragments of that work.
- She will at some point try Lung Constriction herself. The first attempt (Ch4 or Ch5) backfires — she starts burning, screaming, the party stop her. Not fatal. Deeply upsetting. **The second attempt (Ch6) cannot be stopped and kills her.**

## Personality
- Tries to act like things are fine when they are not.
- Quiet, observant, helpful in small ways without being asked.
- Forms attachment slowly but durably. Once she trusts someone she trusts them completely.
- Unbothered by things she has no context for (the symbols, her own dreams) — bothered by things she does (her parents, being a burden).

## Notes
- **The drawing of the party** that she pins on the wall in Ch6 stays there from then on. Mara never takes it down. It is never mentioned again. It is always there.
- Players should feel fond of her by Ch5 without thinking about it. That's the trick.
