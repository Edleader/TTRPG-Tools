# Arc 2 — TBD Title

## Overview
Brad re-enters the picture. He survived the anomaly but gained no powers. Having spent his whole life as the group's punching bag, something broke in him. He now seeks control and power through other means.

The arc is built around a slow reveal. Early encounters should leave players genuinely uncertain — is Brad a victim of the new world, or is he doing terrible things? The answer is the latter, but it shouldn't be obvious until late Arc 2.

## Themes
- Old relationships under new pressures
- Power without ability
- Manipulation and control
- Trust and betrayal
